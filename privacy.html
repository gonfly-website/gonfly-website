Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective date: 30 October 2026 · Last updated: 30 October 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how GoNfly handles personal information when you visit gonflytravel.com, contact us, create an account, submit an enquiry or use our visa and travel assistance services. Please do not send passport scans, bank statements, card details or other sensitive documents through an ordinary website message or email unless we have specifically provided a secure method and instructions.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

“GoNfly”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the GoNfly travel and visa assistance service operating through gonflytravel.com. Our website lists our office location as Rohini, New Delhi, India.

For privacy questions, requests about your information or complaints, contact our privacy/grievance contact at gonfly.info@gmail.com. Please include your name, a way for us to identify the relevant enquiry or account, and a description of your request. Do not email identity documents unless we ask for them through an appropriate secure channel.

GoNfly should be identified in customer-facing contracts and invoices by its full legal business name and complete postal address. If these differ from the brand name or office locality above, those details should be supplied in the relevant service documents.

2. Personal information we may collect

  • Contact and enquiry information: name, mobile number, email address, destination, service requested, travel dates, number of travellers, budget range and messages you choose to provide.
  • Account information: name, email, phone number, authentication identifiers and account/profile details when you register or sign in to the customer portal.
  • Application and service information: travel purpose, nationality, residence, applicant details, information you provide for a visa or travel enquiry, application reference, status, quotations, staff communications and service history.
  • Identity and travel documents: passport, photograph, itinerary, accommodation, financial or supporting documents only where you choose to provide them or they are requested for a specific service. Do not send these through an unsecured channel.
  • Payment and transaction information: payment status, amount, transaction reference and billing details if a payment method is introduced for a service. GoNfly does not ask you to send full card numbers, CVV or banking passwords in a message.
  • Technical information: device/browser information, IP address, access times, pages viewed, diagnostic and security logs, and cookie or similar technology information where used by the website or its hosting/security providers.
  • Communications: emails, calls, WhatsApp messages and other correspondence with our team, including information needed to respond to your request.

Please provide only information that is relevant and accurate. If you provide information about another traveller, you confirm that you are authorised to share it and have informed that person where required.

3. Why we use your information

We use personal information only for relevant business and service purposes, including:

  • responding to enquiries and providing visa, travel planning and related assistance;
  • assessing the information you provide and preparing document checklists, quotations or service recommendations;
  • creating and maintaining customer accounts, applications, reference numbers and service records;
  • communicating with you about requirements, missing information, appointments, status changes and service delivery;
  • where you instruct us, submitting or sharing relevant information with embassies, consulates, visa application centres, airlines, hotels, insurers, appointment platforms, government authorities or other service providers involved in your requested service;
  • processing payments, accounting, invoicing, fraud prevention and dispute handling where applicable;
  • maintaining website security, preventing misuse, troubleshooting and improving our services;
  • meeting legal, regulatory, tax, record-keeping or lawful government requirements; and
  • sending service-related updates. Promotional messages will be sent where permitted, and you can ask us to stop them.

We do not use your passport, financial or visa-application information to make unrelated advertising profiles.

4. Consent, choice and required information

We ask for information that is reasonably needed for the enquiry or service you request. Where consent is the appropriate basis for processing, we will seek it in a clear manner and use the information for the stated purpose. You may withdraw consent by contacting us at the address above. Withdrawal does not make earlier processing unlawful and may mean that we cannot continue a service that depends on the information.

Some information may be required to provide a requested service or comply with law. If you do not provide it, we may be unable to assess the enquiry, submit an application or complete the service. We will explain this where reasonably possible.

5. When we share information

We may share only the information reasonably needed for the relevant purpose with:

  • embassies, consulates, immigration authorities, visa application centres and authorised appointment or submission channels;
  • airlines, hotels, tour operators, insurers, ground handlers and other travel suppliers when you request their services;
  • technology and operations providers supporting hosting, website security, customer accounts, communications, data storage or enquiry management;
  • professional advisers, auditors, insurers and payment service providers where relevant; and
  • government, law-enforcement or regulatory bodies where disclosure is required or legally permitted.

These recipients may process information under their own privacy terms and legal obligations. Government authorities and visa application centres make their own decisions and may retain information under their rules. GoNfly does not control their independent processing.

6. Technology providers, hosting and transfers

GoNfly's website infrastructure may use service providers for website hosting and security, application programming, customer authentication, database/storage services and enquiry workflows. These providers process information on our behalf or as independent providers according to the services configured by GoNfly.

Some providers or travel/visa recipients may process information outside the state or country where you are located. Where personal information is transferred across borders, we will apply the requirements and restrictions of applicable Indian law and any other applicable law.

We do not publish a complete list of technology vendors in this policy because the active production configuration can change. Contact us if you need information about the providers involved in a particular service.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how visitors use the website and improve its content and performance. GA4 may collect information such as pages viewed, interactions, approximate location derived from IP address, device and browser details, referral source and usage events. Google may use cookies or similar identifiers for this purpose. We use these insights for website measurement and service improvement, not to make visa eligibility decisions. Google processes data under its own terms and privacy practices. You can learn more at Google's Privacy Policy.

Our website and service providers may also use essential technologies to operate pages, remember settings, maintain account sessions and protect against abuse. enabled, we will provide appropriate notice and choices where required by law. You can also manage cookies through your browser; disabling essential cookies may affect website functions.

8. How long we keep information

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including completing the requested service, maintaining customer and transaction records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements and meeting legal or regulatory obligations. Retention can differ by record type and legal requirement. When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or remove identifying details, subject to lawful retention, backups and technical constraints.

We do not promise a single fixed deletion period for every category of information. You may contact us to ask about information associated with your account or enquiry.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse. No website, email, messaging service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Access to customer information should be limited to people who need it for their assigned work. Please protect your account credentials and notify us promptly if you suspect unauthorised account access.

10. Your choices and privacy requests

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to request access to information associated with you, correction or updating of inaccurate information, deletion where we no longer need to retain it, withdrawal of consent, or information about how your data is being used or shared. You may also object to or opt out of promotional communications.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request and may retain limited records where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. We will explain if a request cannot be fully completed and why.

11. Children and other travellers

Our services may involve travel enquiries for minors, but a parent or lawful guardian should provide information and act on behalf of a child where required. We do not knowingly seek a child's personal information for unrelated purposes. If you believe a child has provided information to us without appropriate authorisation, contact us so we can review it.

12. Third-party websites and services

Our website may link to third-party websites, booking systems, government portals, payment services or social platforms. Their content, security and privacy practices are not controlled by GoNfly. Review their terms and privacy notices before submitting information to them.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, systems or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date. Material changes may also be communicated through the website or directly where appropriate.

14. Complaints and grievance contact

For a privacy concern or grievance, email gonfly.info@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy Request” or “Privacy Grievance”. We will acknowledge and review the request and respond within the period required by applicable law. If a statutory grievance or appeal mechanism applies, you may use the mechanism available under the applicable law.
