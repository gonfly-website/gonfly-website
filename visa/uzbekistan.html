Frequently Asked Questions

Do Indian citizens need a visa to visit Uzbekistan?

Visa requirements can change and depend on nationality and travel purpose. Check the current official visa guidance before applying. Eligible Indian travellers may use the official e-Visa procedure where applicable.

What documents are required?

Start with the checklist above. Additional documents may be required based on your profile and current immigration rules.

How long does an e-Visa take to process?

The reference processing time is 6 days. Actual processing may vary and is not guaranteed.

How long is the visa valid?

The reference validity is 90 days. Validity and permitted duration of stay may differ; follow the issued visa conditions.

Do I need to print my e-Visa?

Review the current official instructions and carry the travel documents required by the issuing authority and carrier.

Do I need to register my stay?

Registration requirements can depend on accommodation and current local rules. Confirm requirements with your accommodation provider or official authorities.
